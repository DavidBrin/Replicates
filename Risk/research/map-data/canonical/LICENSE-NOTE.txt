classic-world.json — the verified canonical Risk classic world map (42 territories, 6 continents, 83 edges).

LICENCE STATUS: no licence restriction applies to this file's substance.

WHY
- The contents are FACTS about a published board game: territory names, which territory
  borders which, how many armies each continent is worth. Facts and discovered
  information are not copyrightable (no originality in a factual border list).
- The file was not copied from any single source. It was BUILT by diffing nine
  independent open-source adjacency lists and adjudicating the five edges they
  disagreed on. See the "verification" and "adjudications" blocks inside the JSON.
- Seven of those nine sources agree with this graph exactly, and they are variously
  MIT, Apache-2.0, CC0 and Unlicense — all permissive. The two that disagreed were
  overruled, so nothing was taken from them.
